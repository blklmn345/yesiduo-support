Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-31
Operator: HS Chan
Contact: dev@blacklemon.dev

YesIDuo turns your iPhone Duo into a ring box for a proposal.

What you enter in the app

Initials, your partner's name, your message and your choices of case colour, ring and effects are stored on your device. They are never sent to the operator, who runs no server that receives them.

There is no account, no advertising, no ad tracking and no third-party analytics SDK.

In-app purchase

Buying and restoring the 3D presentation is handled by Apple's App Store. Payment details are not shared with the operator. Purchase status is checked with Apple's StoreKit and handled under Apple's policies.

Backups

YesIDuo has no cloud sync of its own. Depending on your device and system settings, app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. The operator never receives those backups.

Deletion

You can change what you entered at any time on the setup screen. To remove all app data from the device, delete the app.

Contact and this website

If you email us, the operator handles your address and message to reply and investigate, keeps them only as long as needed, and then deletes them. Messages are never used for advertising or to train external AI.

This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies of its own.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. For privacy questions, email dev@blacklemon.dev.
