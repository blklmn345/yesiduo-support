How it works

With the device open, choose the case colour, the ring, the words engraved inside the lid and your message, tap "準備完了" (Ready), then close the device and hand it over. The closed outer display shows a ring box; when your partner opens the device like a laptop, the box opens and the ring appears.

Rotation lock

Turn on Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Centre before handing the device over, so the screen doesn't rotate at the big moment.

3D Ring Box (in-app purchase)

The 3D presentation is a one-time in-app purchase. After changing devices or reinstalling, choose "3D" under Display Style on the setup screen and tap "購入を復元" (Restore Purchase). Family Sharing is supported.

Does it work on other iPhones?

Yes, but the opening moment is designed for iPhone Duo.

Where are names and messages stored?

Only on your device. They are never sent to the operator's servers.
